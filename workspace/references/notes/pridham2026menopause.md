# Pridham, Hayut et al. (2026) — Menopause physiological cliff at the FMP

- **BibTeX key:** `pridham2026menopause`
- **Journal:** Nature Medicine, 2026. DOI [10.1038/s41591-026-04614-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04614-0)
- **Authors:** Glen Pridham & Yoav Hayut (co-first), Noa Lavi-Shoseyov, Michal Neeman, Noa Hovav, Yoel Toledano, Uri Alon (corresponding). Weizmann Institute / Clalit / Kaplan / Rabin medical centers.

## Summary

A computational framework — inspired by astronomy deblurring — reconstructs
final-menstrual-period (FMP)-anchored physiological trajectories from
*cross-sectional* medical data by removing the blurring caused by variation in
FMP age across individuals. Applied to two independent cohorts totaling ~300
million lab tests.

## Key findings

- A **step-like physiological cliff at the FMP** across endocrine, skeletal,
  hepatic, renal and lipid systems.
- Discontinuities are **absent in males**, concordant across populations, and
  **markedly attenuated by menopausal hormone therapy**.
- **Dysregulation begins >10 years before FMP.**
- Supports a systemic, quantifiable view of menopause; the deblurring strategy
  generalizes to other transitions (puberty, disease progression).

## Relevance to this workspace

Methodological: recovering hidden biological trajectories from cross-sectional
population data — a candidate technique for reconstructing dynamics where only
snapshot/aggregate measurements exist.
