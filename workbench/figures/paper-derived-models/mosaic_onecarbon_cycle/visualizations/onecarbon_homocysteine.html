Faithful recreation of Kim, Nijhout & Reed 2021: the curated Reed2004 methionine-cycle model (BioModels BIOMD0000000698) driven by a menstrual-cycle estradiol input, with estradiol activating CBS via Kim's h(E2). Homocysteine tracks estradiol inversely (corr ≈ −0.94), reaching its minimum at the ovulatory E2 peak around day 12 — the paper's central result.
